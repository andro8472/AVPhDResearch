# Research context

Andrew Vassiliou's PhD research at the University of Surrey is titled **Analysing Film Content: a Text-based Approach**. The work examined textual film representations, including screenplays and audio-description scripts, and used text mining and statistical analysis to extract narrative events and related information.

The supplied Python development archive is organised as a processing workflow: acquisition, corpus preparation, text processing, collocation analysis, sentiment classification, keyword and named-entity exploration, and visualisation.

The archive appears to include later exploratory code as well as historical research material. **Do not assume every script or experiment formed part of the original PhD.** Source-specific provenance, publication dates, evaluation metrics and research results should be documented before attributing them to the thesis.
